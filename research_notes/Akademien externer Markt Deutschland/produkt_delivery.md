## 1. Portfolio logic: core products vs. long tail, certificate courses vs. single seminars, learning paths, flat rates; which formats sell in B2B

### Takeaway
In Germany, in-person (Präsenz) training is still the most-used format, but live-online already accounts for about a quarter of the market and blended formats for about 15%. Experts rate blended learning as the top learning form. For a new academy, that points to a small core of blended or live-online certificate courses (Lehrgänge) with standard building blocks. A long tail of single seminars and e-learning flat rates can then come from licensed content instead of being built in-house.

### Cited Findings
- **Lünendonk market study (Sept 2025, 12th edition after a 15-year pause):** in-person events are still the most-used format. Live-online courses already hold about **26%**, and blended formats (in-person plus online phases) about **15%** (verify: the extract was patchy). The study names digital transformation and the skilled-labour shortage as demand drivers. The PDF costs €2,400 plus VAT. — [Lünendonk press release PDF](https://www.luenendonk.de/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/LUE_WB_2.PI_f.pdf); [Lünendonk Weiterbildung page](https://www.luenendonk.de/en/weiterbildung)
- **mmb Trendmonitor 2024/2025** (19th mmb Learning Delphi, survey Dec 2024 to Jan 2025): headline is "Lernen wird intelligenter – Dialogische und KI-unterstützte Lernformen immer wichtiger". Experts expect a strong shift toward individual learning, with collaborative learning also growing. — [mmb Trendmonitor 2024/2025 PDF](https://www.mmb-institut.de/wp-content/uploads/mmb-Trendmonitor_2024-2025.pdf) (not fetched; host blocked)
- **Blended learning** stays among the top learning forms with **83%** expert agreement in the mmb study, according to secondary coverage by Haufe Akademie (verify against the mmb PDF). Haufe also cites mmb as showing blended learning leads in learning success. — [Haufe Akademie Magazin: Blended Learning](https://www.haufe-akademie.de/digitales-lernen/magazin/blended-learning-warum-es-sich-lohnt). Haufe is a provider with an interest in this finding.
- Earlier mmb editions (2022/23) also ranked blended learning first, a trend for 10+ years. — [blink.it summary Trendmonitor 2023](https://www.blink.it/en/blog/trendmonitor-2023)
- **TÜV Rheinland survey:** 56% of HR managers see a mix of in-person and online (blended) as giving the greatest learning success. Year not clearly stated. — [ZfK report](https://www.zfk.de/uncategorized/tuev-rheinland-nachhaltigkeit-wird-top-thema-in-der-weiterbildung)
- **TÜV-Verband Weiterbildungsstudie 2022:** live-online trainings ranked 3rd in preferences at 73%. For business and management topics, in-person leads narrowly (43%) over live-online (40%). Older data. — via search extract of [presseportal](https://www.presseportal.de/pm/65031/5352930) (verify)
- **WIFI Austria 2025** (firms with 10+ employees): in-person is still important, but the in-person/online mix is established. Austrian data. — [WIFI press PDF 2025](https://www.wifi.at/uploads/Pres_WWB_Pressegespräch_2025_V2.pdf)
- **Flat-rate / library model benchmark (Haufe Akademie Content Collection):** flat-rate licences in quotas starting at **50 flat licences** plus a base rent. Marketing price point "E-Learnings ab 1,67 € im Monat" per licence. **[VENDOR CLAIM]** — [Haufe Content Collection](https://www.haufe-akademie.de/digital-suite/content-collection); [Haufe "Angebot Flex"](https://www.haufe-akademie.de/digitales-lernen/lerninhalte/angebot-flex)
- **LinkedIn Workplace Learning Report 2025:** only 36% of organisations are "career development champions" with robust programmes, 31% have programmes with limited adoption, and 33% have none or are just starting. AI-literacy skills on profiles grew 177% versus a 36% rise in all listed skills. Champions are "42% more likely" to be gen-AI frontrunners; secondary sources phrase this metric inconsistently. — [LinkedIn WLR 2025 PDF](https://learning.linkedin.com/content/dam/me/business/en-us/talent-solutions/resources/pdfs/linkedIn-workplace-learning-report-2025.pdf); secondary: [Absorb summary](https://www.absorblms.com/blog/linkedin-workplace-learning-report-takeaways), [getAbstract summary](https://www.getabstract.com/en/blog/linkedin-s-2025-workplace-learning-report)
- **Learning-path packaging in the market:** IHK academies run blended certificate courses (e.g. IHK Nordschwarzwald "Blended-Learning-Angebote"). — [IHK Nordschwarzwald](https://www.ihk.de/nordschwarzwald/akademie-fuer-weiterbildung/blended-learning-angebote-4789240)

### Inferences
- **Core products (around 5 to 8):** multi-day certificate courses (Zertifikatslehrgänge) in blended or live-online format on high-demand topics (AI literacy, digital transformation, leadership, compliance). They carry higher prices, a credential and better repeat bookability. Live-online removes room and travel costs and scales nationally.
- **Long tail:** single seminars and short online formats (2 to 4 h) as entry products and lead generators, plus a licensed e-learning library sold as a flat rate per seat (the Haufe model shows the B2B market accepts per-seat flat rates). The long tail should be curated, not produced in-house.
- **Learning paths:** bundle single modules into a path that ends in a certificate or badge. This lets one module be sold several times (as a single seminar and as part of a path).
- For B2B in-house business, the evidence suggests offering a format menu (in-person, live-online, blended) for the same content. In-person remains the default expectation for leadership and soft-skills topics.

### Gaps
- No primary-source numbers on which formats generate the most **revenue** (as opposed to usage or preference) in German B2B; Lünendonk's full study is paid.
- The detailed mmb 2024/25 ranking tables could not be read (host blocked). The 83% blended figure is secondary.
- No found data on "Lernreisen" (learning journeys) demand or pricing; no Training Industry (US) data was retrieved.
- No found 2024–2026 Haufe/learnchamp study with format-preference percentages. A Haufe "PE-Umfrage 2025" exists, but its content was not retrieved — [Haufe PE-Umfrage 2025](https://www.haufe-akademie.de/info-center/form/studie-pe-umfrage-2025-pdf).

## 2. Credentials as value drivers: IHK/DIHK certificates, university certificates/ECTS, microcredentials, Open Badges, ISO/IEC 17024

### Takeaway
The fastest credential with real brand value is an **IHK certificate course licensed via the DIHK-Bildungs-gGmbH and a regional IHK cooperation**, which now comes with DIHK Open Badges. ECTS microcredentials need a university partner and take longer. Your own Open Badges can be set up within weeks. ISO/IEC 17024 personnel certification is the most credible option but the slowest and most expensive, so it does not fit a 90-day launch.

### Cited Findings
**IHK/DIHK**
- Standardised nationwide IHK certificate courses are developed by the DIHK-Bildungs-gGmbH with the chambers, then delivered regionally. Regional providers can secure usage rights for an IHK district by contract. In the Kassel-Marburg example the provider must have its registered office in the district, the topic must be relevant to regional firms, and the provider signs a cooperation agreement with the IHK. — [IHK Kassel-Marburg: IHK-Zertifikate](https://www.ihk.de/kassel-marburg/hauptnavigation/ausbildung-und-weiterbildung/weiterbildung/ihk-zertifikate-4105520) (search extract; page fetch blocked)
- Example of a course delivered "in Kooperation mit der DIHK-Bildungs-GmbH". — [IHK Köln: Sustainable Kommunikation](https://www.ihk.de/koeln/hauptnavigation/weiterbildung/zertifikatslehrgaenge/sustainable-kommunikation-5694100)
- The DIHK-Bildungs-gGmbH is a non-profit subsidiary of the DIHK in Berlin; the correct name is "gGmbH". — [DIHK: DIHK-Bildungs-gGmbH](https://www.dihk.de/de/ueber-uns/gesellschaften/dihk-bildungs-ggmbh)
- **IHK Open Badges:** issued for DIHK courses since June 2023 and for regional IHK Erfurt courses from early 2024, as a supplement to the paper certificate. — [IHK Erfurt press release](https://www.ihk.de/erfurt/presse/pressemitteilungen/digitale-leistungsnachweise-fuer-regionale-ihk-lehrgaenge-6027570)
- IHK Nürnberg uses the nationwide DIHK-Bildungs-gGmbH Open Badges system for its certificate courses. — [IHK Nürnberg WiM 2024](https://www.ihk-nuernberg.de/wim-wirtschaft-in-mittelfranken/ihk-magazin-wim-06-072024/details/lehrgangszertifikat-wird-digital)
- IHK Gießen-Friedberg introduced Open Badges for certificate courses from 2025. — [IHK Gießen-Friedberg](https://www.ihk.de/giessen-friedberg/geschaeftsbereiche/bildung/weiterbildung/downloads/open-badges-6482678)
- IHK Berlin sends participants' name and email to the DIHK-Bildungs-gGmbH to issue badges. Badges contain course content and an authenticity verification. — [IHK Berlin Open Badges](https://www.ihk.de/berlin/pruefungen-lehrgaenge/ihk-die-weiterbildung/open-badges-digitales-zertifikat-6124594)
- IHK pages say badges can be embedded in websites, social profiles and email signatures, and are verifiable through metadata. — [IHK Rhein-Neckar](https://www.ihk.de/rhein-neckar/ausbildung-weiterbildung/weiterbildung-channel/beratung/open-badges); [IHK Darmstadt](https://www.ihk.de/darmstadt/produktmarken/weiterbildung/open-badges-5983066)

**EU microcredentials / university ECTS**
- The EU Council Recommendation on a European approach to micro-credentials was adopted on 16 June 2022. It sets standard elements (learning outcomes, workload in ECTS where possible, assessment, quality assurance and others) and principles for transparency, quality assurance, recognition and relevance. The official reference, Council Recommendation 2022/C 243/02 (OJ C 243, 27.6.2022), comes from background knowledge and was not fetched. The German Bundesrat discussed the proposal on 20 May 2022 and pointed out that modular offers such as Teilqualifikationen already exist in Germany. — [Bundesrat Drucksache 14/22(B)](https://dserver.bundestag.de/brd/2022/0014-22B.pdf)
- DAAD surveys of German universities on microcredentials: 155 responses in 2022 and 119 in 2023. Offers range from 1–5, 1–15 or 1–30 ECTS. — [DAAD press release 2023](https://www.daad.de/de/pressemitteilungen/microcredentials-an-deutschen-hochschulen-immer-beliebter_2023/); [DAAD EU kick-off 2023 slides](https://eu.daad.de/medien/eu.daad.de.2016/dokumente/service/veranstaltungen/2023/kick-off_meeting_2023_tag2.pdf)
- Example: HAWK's Hornemann Institute offers all its online courses also as ECTS microcredentials. — [HAWK press release](https://exik.hawk.de/en/news-portal/press-releases/hornemann-institute-now-also-offers-all-online-courses-micro-credentials)
- Austria's position defines microcredentials at 3–15 ECTS. The EU recommendation leaves size open. — [BMBWF Austria Bologna follow-up recommendation](https://www.BMBWF.gv.at/dam/jcr:c4037609-de22-418c-b2e6-bcf871f02af9/Empfehlung%20der%20nationalen%20Bologna%20Follow-up%20Gruppe%20zur%20Umsetzung%20von%20Micro-credentials%20in%20%C3%96sterreich.pdf)
- KI-Campus discussion paper on microcredentials and micro-degrees (2022). — [KI-Campus](https://ki-campus.org/sites/default/files/2022-09/2022-08-Diskussionspapier_Micro-Credentials_Micro-Degrees_KI-Campus.pdf)

**ISO/IEC 17024 personnel certification**
- In Germany, DAkkS accredits certification bodies for persons under DIN EN ISO/IEC 17024 and lists them in a public database. — [DAkkS: certification bodies for persons](https://dakks.de/en/certification-bodies-for-persons.html)
- Accredited bodies remain under DAkkS surveillance with regular checks. Fraunhofer states that DAkkS verifies its processes annually. — [Fraunhofer Personenzertifizierung](https://www.personenzertifizierung.fraunhofer.de/en.html); [TÜV SÜD Kompetenzzertifikat](https://www.tuvsud.com/de-de/store/akademie/personenzertifizierung/kompetenzzertifikat-qualitaetsmanagement)
- A newer edition, ISO/IEC 17024:2026, appears alongside the 2012 edition (verify). — [SFS store](https://store.sfs.fi/en/isoiec-17024-2012)

**Trainer credentials as quality signal**
- BDVT runs examination frameworks for "Fachtrainer", "Business-Trainer" and "Online-Trainer". Training providers commit to its quality standards, and BDVT samples participant satisfaction. The Online-Trainer exam includes a 30-minute live-online sequence. — [BDVT Ausbildungsrahmen Fachtrainer](https://www.bdvt.de/weblication/grid5/tmpHTTP/_download_65e7d6df73357dc9e95d6b38585fc614/Ausbildungsrahmen-Fachtrainer.pdf); [BDVT Prüfungsrahmen Online-Trainer](https://www.bdvt.de/de-wAssets/docs/akademieangebote/Online-Trainer/Pruefungsrahmen_BDVT_Online_Trainer_in_final.pdf)

### Inferences (pros, cons and time to set up)
| Credential | Value driver | Cons | Rough time to set up (inferred) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Own academy certificate plus Open Badge | Fast, full control, LinkedIn-shareable | Low brand value at first | 2–6 weeks (badge platform plus design) |
| IHK certificate course (DIHK-Bildungs-gGmbH, licensed via regional IHK) | Strong brand recognition in German SMEs; DIHK badges included | Regional exclusivity and office requirement, fixed curricula, licence fees not published, dependency on IHK | Likely months (cooperation agreement); not verified |
| University certificate / ECTS microcredential | Academic credit, transferable, fits the EU microcredential frame | Requires university partner, its QA processes and accreditation logic | Likely 6–12+ months (inferred) |
| ISO/IEC 17024 personnel certification | Highest credibility, independent of the training (impartiality) | Requires a separate certification body or a partner such as TÜV or DEKRA; DAkkS accreditation, annual surveillance | Usually a year or more (inferred, no source found) |

- A realistic 90-day path: launch with your own certificate plus Open Badges aligned to the EU microcredential standard elements (learning outcomes, workload, assessment, QA), in parallel start IHK or university partnership talks for a later premium tier, and use partner 17024 schemes (e.g. TÜV or DEKRA certification) where they already exist, instead of building one.

### Gaps
- No published DIHK licence fees, revenue-share terms or national application process were found.
- No figures for DAkkS 17024 accreditation duration or costs. A Bundestag inquiry exists, but the extract had no figures — [BT-Drs. 19/5141](https://dserver.bundestag.de/btd/19/051/1905141.pdf).
- No concrete German federal or KMK implementation rules for microcredentials were found.
- No data on employer willingness to pay more for credentialed versus uncredentialed courses.

## 3. Content sourcing: buy/license, co-create, AI-generated content, quality assurance

### Takeaway
Licensing libraries such as GoodHabitz or Haufe fills the long tail fast, but content is usually **not customisable** and often not white-label. AI video tools (Synthesia, HeyGen) can cut production time and cost a lot, but every quantitative claim found comes from the vendors themselves. Core products should be co-created with expert trainers and checked through a light internal QA process.

### Cited Findings
- **GoodHabitz:** its content is standardised and "delivered as-is", so the training content cannot be customised. Custom learning paths are possible only through LMS integration. **[VENDOR CLAIM]** — [GoodHabitz compare page](https://www.goodhabitz.com/compare); [GoodHabitz LMS content](https://goodhabitz.com/product/lms)
- GoodHabitz runs a partner programme and is described by partners as "a key content partner with premium localized content" in 20+ languages. **[VENDOR CLAIM]** — [GoodHabitz global partnerships](https://www.goodhabitz.com/global-partnerships); [360Learning marketplace](https://360learning.com/partner-marketplace/goodhabitz/)
- GoodHabitz content is also resold via OpenSesame, so marketplace reselling exists. — [OpenSesame GoodHabitz](https://www.opensesame.com/fl/s/goodhabitz)
- Speexx (language training with AI and certified coaches) is listed as a 360Learning partner integration. **[VENDOR CLAIM]** — [360Learning partners](https://360learning.com/fr/blog/partenaires-plateformes-integrations/)
- Haufe Content Collection (German competitor and potential supplier) uses a flat-rate model starting at 50 licences plus base rent. **[VENDOR CLAIM]** — [Haufe Content Collection](https://www.haufe-akademie.de/digital-suite/content-collection)
- **Synthesia:** a Zoom case study claims training videos are made **90% faster** and subject experts save 15–20 h per month. A Paragon Skills case study claims **95% cost savings**. **[VENDOR CLAIM]** — [Synthesia Zoom case (DE)](https://www.synthesia.io/de/case-studies/zoom); [Synthesia Paragon Skills case](https://www.synthesia.io/case-studies/paragon-skills)
- **HeyGen:** an ROI calculator and customer quotes (Sibelco "€1,000 saved per minute of training video"; Würth "80% lower video translation costs"). **[VENDOR CLAIM]** — [HeyGen ROI calculator](https://www.heygen.com/de-de/video-roi-calculator)
- An independent 30-day Synthesia review (2026) notes that pricing information conflicts: Creator tier from about US$64, but some features only in custom Enterprise plans. — [eesel.ai Synthesia review](https://www.eesel.ai/de/blog/synthesia-review)
- The mmb Trendmonitor 2024/25 headline stresses AI-supported and dialogical learning forms gaining importance. — [mmb Trendmonitor 2024/2025](https://www.mmb-institut.de/wp-content/uploads/mmb-Trendmonitor_2024-2025.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Make/buy matrix:** core certificate courses are co-created with 1–2 senior expert trainers (IP owned by the academy and licensed back to the trainer if needed). Long-tail self-paced content is licensed. AI tools speed up microlearning, recaps, translations and update videos, not the live parts.
- Because libraries such as GoodHabitz are not customisable, a library resold under the academy's brand is less differentiated. Use it as a flat-rate add-on, not as the main brand promise.
- **Quality assurance:** a lightweight version is enough at the start. Use a four-eyes review of learning outcomes (fits the EU microcredential element "learning outcomes"), a pilot run with feedback, standard evaluation (Kirkpatrick Level 1–2), and a trainer briefing. The BDVT quality frameworks are a reference for trainer-side standards.
- Rights, data protection (GDPR) and labelling of AI-generated avatars or voices must be cleared. This is an inference; no German legal source was retrieved here.

### Gaps
- No public white-label or reseller margins for GoodHabitz, Speexx or LinkedIn Learning were found; they need direct quotes.
- No independent (non-vendor) studies on AI video learning effectiveness or cost savings were found.
- Articulate (Rise/AI Assistant) and open content (OER, e.g. KI-Campus) licence terms were not researched in detail.

## 4. Trainer model: freelancer pools, fees, selection and qualification, train-the-trainer, revenue share

### Takeaway
A variable freelancer pool keeps fixed costs low. Day rates in Germany vary widely: public-sector scales are about €300–570 per day, and market benchmarks (not verified) are about €800–1,500. The big 2024–2027 risk is **Scheinselbstständigkeit**: since the BSG "Herrenberg" ruling, freelance teachers are often classed as employees, and the § 127 SGB IV transition rule only provides protection until (as extended) 31.12.2027 and only with documented consent.

### Cited Findings
**Fees**
- **DAAD (BIDS, 03/2024):** lecturer fees for a full day are €300–367 for non-academic roles and €350–566 where academic qualification is required; moderation is €200–250 per day. — [DAAD Honorarsätze](https://www2.daad.de/downloads/foerderprogramm/file.php?id=8252)
- **Erfurt municipal fee scale (from 1 Jan 2024):** example €557 per full day at 100% (7+ hours billed as a full day). — [Erfurt Honorarstaffel 2024](https://Erfurt.de/mam/ef/rathaus/buergerservice/doc/honorarstaffel_2024.pdf)
- **fratch.io benchmark:** average trainer day rate about €962 in Germany, from €786 (IT trainer) to €1,505 (sales trainer). Data source and date unclear; treat with caution. — [fratch.io rate benchmark](https://fratch.io/en/role/trainer/germany/rate-benchmark)
- **managerSeminare "WeiterbildungsSzene Deutschland 2023"** (paid fee and salary study for trainers, consultants and coaches) notes that the pandemic changed freelancers' billing models. The figures were not accessible. — [managerSeminare Leseprobe](https://teaser.managerseminare.de/leseproben/KPtb-12046_57070.pdf)
- **BDU Honorarstudie 2025 (consulting, not training):** wide spread of fees and a falling price level, with day rates under pressure. — [BDU Musterseiten Honorarstudie 2025](https://www.bdu.de/fileadmin/user_data/dokumente/Studien/Musterseiten_BDU_Honorarstudie_2025.pdf)
- BDVT published a "Honorarempfehlung für Training, Beratung und Coaching 2024" (figures not retrieved). Berlin also has a 2020 fee recommendation for training and coaching. — [Bildungsserver: Honorare in der Weiterbildung](https://www.bildungsserver.de/Honorare-in-der-Weiterbildung-5307-de.html); [Berlin Honorarempfehlung 2020](https://www.berlin.de/sen/europa/_assets/srvice/projektfoerderung/honorarempfehlung-training-coaching_2020.pdf)

**Selection and qualification**
- BDVT "geprüfte Fachtrainer/Business-Trainer/Online-Trainer" frameworks: providers commit to quality standards, and the online-trainer exam includes a 30-minute live-online sequence assessed by BDVT. — [BDVT Leitlinie Online-Trainer](https://www.bdvt.de/de-wAssets/docs/akademieangebote/Online-Trainer/Leitlinie_BDVT_Online_Trainer_in_final.pdf); [BDVT Ausbildungsrahmen Business-Trainer](https://www.bdvt.de/bdvt-wAssets/docs/Akademie/Ausbildungsrahmen-Business-Trainer-in.pdf)
- Haufe Akademie's Fachtrainer programme ends with certification by BDVT and Haufe jointly, which is a ready-made train-the-trainer route. **[VENDOR]** — [Haufe Akademie Fachtrainer](https://www.haufe-akademie.de/33710)

**Scheinselbstständigkeit risk (critical for the freelancer model)**
- BSG ruling of 28.06.2022 (B 12 R 3/20 R, "Herrenberg") called the self-employed status of freelance teachers into question. — [Deloitte Tax News](https://www.deloitte-tax-news.de/arbeitnehmerentsendung-personal/sozialversicherung/uebergangsregelung-zur-sozialversicherungspflicht-von-honorar-lehrkraeften-auswirkungen-auf-bildungseinrichtungen.html)
- § 127 SGB IV transition rule, in force since 1 March 2025, originally ran until 31.12.2026. It requires the teacher's explicit consent in the fee contract. — [Grant Thornton](https://www.grantthornton.de/themen/2025/sozialgesetzbuch-die-neue-uebergangsregelung-fuer-lehrtaetigkeiten/); [otto-schmidt Arbeitsrecht-Blog](https://www.otto-schmidt.de/blog/arbeitsrecht-blog/bundestag-korrigiert-herrenberg-urteil-des-bsg-durch-ubergangsregelung-fur-lehrtatigkeiten-ARBLOG0008456.html)
- **Extension:** the Bundestag decided on 5.3.2026 to extend the rule to **31.12.2027**. The Haufe headline says "auf 2028 verschoben", meaning the duty starts in 2028; verify the final wording. — [Haufe öffentlicher Dienst](https://www.haufe.de/oeffentlicher-dienst/sozialrecht/sozialversicherungspflicht-von-lehrkraeften-wird-verschoben_148_641470.html); [IWW 31.07.2026](https://www.iww.de/lgp/sozialversicherung/sozialversicherungspflicht-uebergangsregelung-in-127-abs-1-sgb-iv-erklaerung-der-lehrkraft-auch-noch-in-pruef-und-gerichtsverfahren-moeglich-f175442)
- The wording covers "Lehrtätigkeiten"; it can be argued that it also covers other teaching-type activities. — [Sonntag & Partner](https://www.sonntag-partner.de/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/2025-06-26_127-SGB-IV.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Pool design:** 15–30 vetted freelancers for 5–8 core products, at least 2 trainers per core product to avoid single points of failure. Selection uses a CV check, a demo session (live-online check like the BDVT exam), reference checks and a pilot with an evaluation threshold.
- **Pay models:** a fixed day rate (planning certainty, easier compliance) versus a revenue share or base-plus-bonus per participant above break-even (shares risk, motivates trainer co-marketing). A hybrid with a low fixed fee plus a per-participant bonus suits open-enrolment seminars. In-house B2B usually uses a fixed day rate.
- **Compliance:** use § 127 SGB IV consent clauses in all fee contracts now. Design contracts with real freelancer indicators (trainers' own methods, several clients, no integration into the organisation). Plan for the case where the rule expires at end-2027, for example status determination procedures (Statusfeststellung) or a small employed trainer core for the highest-volume products. This is inference; get legal advice.
- **Train-the-trainer:** a short internal onboarding (didactic standards, platform, academy brand, evaluation). Optionally sponsor BDVT Online-Trainer certification for core trainers.

### Gaps
- No current (2024–2026) managerSeminare or BDVT fee figures were retrievable; the studies are paywalled.
- No data on typical revenue-share percentages between academies and trainers in DACH.

## 5. Tech for selling: LMS/academy platforms with e-commerce, booking and seminar management, payment, participant management, certificates

### Takeaway
The German market has many seminar management suites (edoobox, KuferSQL, ANTRAGO, SEMCO, simplyOrg, cmxOrganize, TCmanager and others) and German-hosted webinar tools (edudip). Almost all evidence is vendor-provided. A lean stack combines a booking and seminar management tool (catalogue, online booking, invoicing, participant and trainer management, certificate generation), a GDPR-compliant live-online tool, an LMS for blended or self-paced parts, and an Open Badge issuer.

### Cited Findings
- In a Softguide RFP for a health seminar provider, edoobox scored highest (25/25 functions) alongside Fairgate and simplyOrg. This was a single RFP scoring, not an independent test. — [Softguide Ausschreibung](https://www.softguide.de/ausschreibungen/seminaranbieterin-sucht-software-fuer-die-seminarverwaltung)

## 6. Launching an MVP portfolio in 90 days: validation, pre-sales, waitlists, minimum viable seminar, unit economics and break-even

### Takeaway
No German source gives a ready-made 90-day academy launch playbook or benchmark seminar margins. The usable evidence is the market's own mechanics: minimum participant numbers (around 15 in examples), cost categories from AZAV calculation practice, and live-online as a low-fixed-cost format. Break-even must be calculated per product with the standard formula.

### Cited Findings
- Market practice: seminars run only above a minimum participant count (example VSSE webinar: minimum **15** participants); other providers set a maximum of about 15. — [Fruitnet / VSSE Webseminare](https://www.fruitnet.com/fruchthandel/vsse-zwei-webseminare-greifen-kostenkalkulation-auf/245004.article)
- AZAV calculation practice lists cost categories to include: room costs, participant selection, QA and evaluation, overheads, and pro-rata preparation and follow-up. — [certqua Brennpunkt AZAV 2025](https://certqua.de/pdf/training/Produktflyer/Brennpunkt_AZAV_2025.pdf)
- Live-online has about a 26% market share (Lünendonk 2025), so it is an accepted B2B format with no room or catering cost. — [Lünendonk PDF](https://www.luenendonk.de/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/LUE_WB_2.PI_f.pdf)
- Trainer day rates as the main fixed cost: €300–566 on public scales, about €962 on an unverified market average. — [DAAD](https://www2.daad.de/downloads/foerderprogramm/file.php?id=8252); [fratch.io](https://fratch.io/en/role/trainer/germany/rate-benchmark)

### Inferences (frameworks for the report writer, not sourced data)
- **Break-even per run:** BE participants = fixed costs per run ÷ (net price per participant − variable costs per participant).
  - Fixed costs include the trainer fee (1 day × €900–1,500 incl. prep) plus room/catering or a live-online tool share, a marketing share, and admin time.
  - Variable costs include materials, certificate or badge, payment fees and catering per person.
  - *Illustrative example (not sourced):* a live-online 1-day seminar at €690 net, with €1,200 trainer fee + €300 marketing and admin = €1,500 fixed and about €40 variable per person, breaks even at 1,500 ÷ 650 ≈ **3 participants**. An in-person day with €1,200 trainer + €800 room + €400 marketing = €2,400 fixed and €90 variable per person (catering) breaks even at 2,400 ÷ 600 ≈ **4 participants**. A target of 8–12 gives a healthy contribution margin.
- **90-day plan (inferred):**
  - Weeks 1–3: pick 3–5 core topics from customer interviews (10–20 HR/L&D buyers) and existing network demand, write one-page product sheets, and set prices.
  - Weeks 3–6: sign 1–2 trainers per product, build a landing page, set up booking software, collect waitlist and pre-registration (letters of intent from pilot customers, early-bird pricing).
  - Weeks 6–10: run a "minimum viable seminar" pilot (live-online, discounted, with 2–3 pilot companies as in-house runs) and collect evaluations and testimonials.
  - Weeks 10–13: open enrolment for the first dates with a go/no-go rule at the minimum participant count, and add a licensed library flat rate as an upsell.
- **Go/no-go rules:** set a decision date 2–3 weeks before each event. Merge undersubscribed dates or switch them to live-online. B2B in-house runs (fixed price per day, e.g. 4–6× an open-seminar seat price) carry little risk and validate products.
- A licensed flat rate (Haufe model with 50+ seats) gives recurring revenue but small margins on resale, so it is an add-on, not the core.

### Gaps
- No DACH benchmark data on seminar profit margins, average fill rates, cancellation rates or customer acquisition cost for training providers were found.
- No case studies of German academies launched within 90 days, and no German data on pre-sale or waitlist conversion.
- Market seminar price points (e.g. Haufe Akademie or TÜV 1–2-day seminar prices) were not systematically collected; this would be a useful follow-up.
